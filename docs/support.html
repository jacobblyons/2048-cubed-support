COMMON QUESTIONS

How do the layers work? Three 4×4 layers are stacked in a cube. A swipe left, right, up or down slides every layer at once. A swipe down the screen drops each tile to the layer below it; matching tiles merge when they meet, on the same layer or between layers.

How is the score worked out? Every merge adds its value. When you make 2048, the total is multiplied by a turn bonus, bigger the fewer moves you used, and a pace bonus, bigger the faster you played. A lost game keeps its plain merge points.

What does the red glow mean? A layer's rim turns red as it fills. When it is full and nothing can merge, the game is over.

Where are my scores kept? The high scores list is on your device. Sign in to Game Center to put your best score on the world leaderboard.

I bought Remove Ads and the ads came back. Open the disc in the upper right of the menu and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.

The cube moves when I tilt the phone. That is the gyroscope parallax. Turn it off in Settings if you would rather it held still.
