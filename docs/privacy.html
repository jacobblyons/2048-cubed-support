PRIVACY POLICY

2048 CUBED

HOME · SUPPORT

The short version: 2048 CUBED keeps nothing of yours. The ads it shows are served by Google, and Google collects what any ad network does. Buy Remove Ads and even that stops.

WHAT THE APP COLLECTS

Nothing. There are no accounts and no analytics of ours. Your saved game, settings and local high scores live only on your device, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

ADVERTISING

The free version shows a banner during play and an occasional full-screen ad after a lost game, both served by Google AdMob. To do that, Google's software collects a device identifier, an approximate location derived from your IP address, information about the ads you see and tap, and crash and performance diagnostics. 2048 CUBED never asks for permission to track you, so Google does not receive your advertising identifier and the ads are not personalised across apps. How Google handles this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

REMOVE ADS

Remove Ads is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we receive no payment details. Once bought, the app shows no ads and Google's software is never started, so the collection above stops.

GAME CENTER

If you sign in to Apple's Game Center, your best score is submitted to Apple's leaderboard service under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see or store your Game Center identity or scores on any server of ours; we have no servers.

CHILDREN

The app is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children. It collects no data itself; the advertising described above is Google's, and is not personalised.
